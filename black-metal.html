01 / CONCEPT SYSTEM

THREE ROUTES.NO DUPLICATES.

These studies deliberately test different Black Metal languages. The final collection should not repeat one logo, crop or frame three times and call it variety.

BM-A / PORTRAIT SYSTEMWorking lead

Frostbite Portrait

Direct, high-contrast portraiture with restrained framing. Strongest route for a first wearable piece because Gerrard remains immediately recognisable.

BM-B / RITUAL SYSTEMDevelopment study

Ritual Hound

More formal and ceremonial: symmetrical framing, vertical structure and a larger sense of artefact rather than tour-shirt collage.

BM-C / ATMOSPHERIC SYSTEMDevelopment study

Northern Apparition

Atmospheric and less literal. More negative space, colder image treatment and a quieter garment presence for customers who do not want a full maximum-density print.

02 / CREATIVE CONTROL

SCENE-CREDIBLE.NOT COSTUME.

The collection needs enough genre fluency to feel authentic without becoming a pile of random symbols or an imitation of a specific band.

What belongs

01Gerrard’s likeness remains accurate and central
02Custom lettering designed around the full name
03Print texture that survives on an actual garment
04Composition adapted to front, back and sleeve placement
05Original visual language with controlled IP risk

What gets rejected

01A pet photograph with a font placed underneath
02Repeated near-identical logos presented as new concepts
03Random occult decoration with no compositional role
04Distorted muzzle, ears, eyes or breed characteristics
05Artwork that only works as a novelty personalised gift
