02 / CREATIVE CONTROL

CRUST PUNK,
NOT COSTUME PUNK.

The work should look built through repetition, wear and necessity—not assembled from a ready-made “punk” icon pack.

What belongs

01One-colour, worn screen-print logic
02Patch-shaped hierarchy and practical garment placement
03Hand-cut or hand-painted lettering with a readable pet name
04Controlled off-register texture and photocopy abrasion
05Gerrard’s Shar Pei muzzle, ears and folds kept recognisable

What gets rejected

01Random safety pins, checks and stock anarchy symbols
02Black Metal symmetry or Grindcore flyer layouts reused again
03Fake distress applied without hierarchy or print purpose
04Manifesto text too small to print or read
05AI-distorted anatomy hidden behind “roughness”
