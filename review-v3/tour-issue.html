01 / WHY IT EXISTS

REAL MERCH FIRST.
YOUR PET INSIDE IT.

Tour Issue is a proof of the BestieBoys proposition: start with a credible merchandise composition, then build the pet into that system.

The source photo provides likeness and personality. The finished composition is designed for the garment and is reviewed before anything could move toward production.
