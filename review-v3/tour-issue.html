01 / WHY IT EXISTS

REAL MERCH FIRST.
YOUR PET INSIDE IT.

Tour Issue is a proof of the BestieBoys proposition: start with a credible merchandise composition, then build the pet into that system.

The source photo provides likeness and personality. The finished composition is designed for the garment and is reviewed before anything could move toward production.

02 / PERSONALISATION MODEL

FOUR THINGS IN.
ONE PIECE OUT.

The eventual ordering flow should stay simple for the customer while the art direction remains controlled on the BestieBoys side.

01PHOTOYour clearest pet photo

The image is reference material for likeness, not the finished layout.

02NAMEExactly as it should appear

The pet name becomes part of the composition and typography system.

03DETAILSOnly what earns its place

Dates, nicknames or short lines stay optional and are used only when they improve the piece.

04SCENEChoose the visual world

The finished artwork follows a defined scene language rather than a universal template.
