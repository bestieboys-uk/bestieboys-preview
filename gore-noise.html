02 / CREATIVE CONTROL

GORE NOISE,
NOT GLITCH PRESET.

Image loss must be designed around recognition and print behaviour. Random RGB splitting and generic static do not create scene credibility.

What belongs

01Hard clipping, scan dropout and deliberate tonal collapse
02One or two spot-colour logic with believable ink coverage
03A recognisable Gerrard silhouette, muzzle and folded ears
04Signal codes and waveform structures that support hierarchy
05Damage that changes by garment rather than scaling blindly

What gets rejected

01Stock VHS filters, rainbow RGB splits or fake screen errors
02Grindcore flyer collage reused under a different heading
03Goregrind medical labels or blood effects without purpose
04Grey visual mush that cannot survive screen or DTG printing
05Lost anatomy excused as noise, speed or abstraction
