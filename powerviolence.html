02 / CREATIVE CONTROL

POWERVIOLENCE,
NOT ANOTHER FLYER.

The scene should feel fast because the hierarchy is decisive—not because the page is filled with random photographs, noise textures and unreadable micro-copy.

What belongs

01Oversized blunt type with immediate hierarchy
02One decisive Gerrard crop rather than image clutter
03Short statements that survive real print size
04Hard black, bone and one controlled signal colour
05Shar Pei muzzle, ears and forehead folds kept accurate

What gets rejected

01Grindcore cut-and-paste collage reused unchanged
02Crust patch grids or Black Metal framing
03Generic mosh-pit, skull or barbed-wire filler
04Paragraphs of manifesto text reduced below print legibility
05Animal distortion excused as aggression or speed
