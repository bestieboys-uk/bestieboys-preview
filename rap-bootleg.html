02 / CREATIVE CONTROL

BOOTLEG ENERGY.
ORIGINAL PROPERTY.

The commercial opportunity is the tribute-shirt composition—not somebody else’s identity. Every name, portrait, logo and supporting device must belong to the customer’s pet and to BestieBoys.

What belongs

01Multiple purposeful crops from the same approved pet photos
02Original chrome, airbrush or serif lettering built around the pet name
03Clear hero hierarchy that still reads from several metres away
04Controlled colour separation and believable print coverage
05Shar Pei folds, muzzle, ears and expression kept anatomically accurate

What gets rejected

01Copied artists, album covers, label logos or celebrity likenesses
02Fake luxury marks, trademark parodies or borrowed tour branding
03Generic clouds, roses and halos added without hierarchy
04Too many colours or gradients for the selected fulfilment method
05AI anatomy errors hidden beneath glow, blur or collage
